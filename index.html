A school built on welfare, not just results

Welfare Mission International School is a co-educational, English-medium school affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education and managed by the Welfare Mission Educational Society.

Classes run from Nursery to XII. Older students choose between the PCM and PCB streams, and every child gets room to try drama, quizzes, art, music and more.

Which board is the school affiliated to?

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), affiliation number 2131971, valid until 31 March 2030.

Which classes and streams do you offer?

Nursery to Class XII in English medium. Classes XI and XII offer PCM and PCB.

How do I apply?

Send the enquiry form on the Admissions page or visit the school office at Shivli Road, Maqsoodabad.

What are the fees?

Fees change by class and year. Please ask the school office for the current fee structure.
