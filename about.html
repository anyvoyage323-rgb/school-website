Our school

Welfare Mission International School stands on Shivli Road in Maqsoodabad, Kanpur Nagar. CBSE records show the school opened on 1 April 2012, and it now holds senior secondary status.

The school is unaided and co-educational, managed by the Welfare Mission Educational Society. English is the medium of instruction from Nursery to Class XII.
