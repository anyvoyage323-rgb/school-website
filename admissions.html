Admissions

Four simple steps from enquiry to first day.

  1. Enquire

    Fill the form below or visit the school office.

  2. Submit documents

    Bring the documents listed on this page.

  3. Meet the school

    Interaction or assessment, depending on the class.

  4. Confirm

    Pay the fees and secure the seat.

Documents to bring

  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card of the child
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous report card and transfer certificate (Class II onwards)
  • Parent ID proof

Ask the school office for the current fee structure and seat availability.
