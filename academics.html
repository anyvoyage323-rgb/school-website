Academics

CBSE learning from Nursery to Class XII, in English.

Stages of learning

Pre-primary (Nursery to UKG)

Play-based learning that builds language, number sense and confidence.

Primary (I to V)

English, Hindi, Mathematics and environmental studies, with regular speaking and writing practice.

Middle (VI to VIII)

Science, Social Science and languages, plus debate and creative writing.

Secondary (IX and X)

CBSE curriculum with preparation for the Class X board examination.

Senior secondary streams

Classes XI and XII are offered in two science streams.

PCM

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for students aiming at engineering, architecture and the sciences.

PCB

Physics, Chemistry and Biology for students aiming at medicine, life sciences and allied fields.
